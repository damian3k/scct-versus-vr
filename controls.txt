SCCT Versus VR - Controls (Meta Quest / Touch controllers)
===========================================================

The mod sends keyboard/mouse input to the game, so every action below is the game's own
action on the stock keyboard layout (key shown in brackets). Keep the game window focused
(click it once after starting the game).

"Tap" = quick press. "Hold" = keep pressed for about 0.35 s.


MERCENARY (first person, aiming with the controller)
----------------------------------------------------
Right controller (point)    Aim the weapon - the gun is in your right hand and shoots
                            where you point it (the merc's laser shows the aim)
Right trigger               Fire                              [Left mouse]
Left trigger                Alt-fire / gadget / hold breath   [Right mouse]
Left stick                  Move                              [W A S D]
Left stick click            Walk / run                        [Middle mouse]
Right stick left/right      Turn your body (smooth turn)
Right stick up/down         Switch item / gadget              [Mouse wheel]
Right stick click           Fire mode / flashlight            [E]
Right grip                  Use / interact                    [Shift]
Left grip                   Roll / laser                      [Q]
A                           Jump                              [Space]
B                           Crouch                            [C]
X                           Reload                            [R]
Left hand to the magazine   Reload: bring your left hand to the magazine of the
                            gun (in front of and below the right controller), like
                            changing the magazine by hand     [R]
Y  (tap)                    Vision modes                      [Tab]
Y  (hold)                   Co-op move                        [T]
Menu button                 In-game menu                      [Esc]


MERCENARY ZOOM (Assault Gun sniper mode / binoculars)
-----------------------------------------------------
Eyes to the gun             Zoom on: raise the right controller close to your face
                            (~20 cm) pointing where you look, like putting your eye
                            to the sights                     [B]
Head away from the gun      Zoom off                          [B]
Right controller (move)     Moves the crosshair like a mouse; the view follows it
Right stick up/down         Zoom in / out                     [Mouse wheel]
Left trigger (hold)         Hold breath (steadier aim)        [Right mouse]
Right trigger               Fire                              [Left mouse]

While zoomed, the game's own zoomed view (scope overlay, crosshair, HUD) is shown on a
screen in front of your eyes. Lift your head and the normal VR view comes back.


SPY (third person)
------------------
Right controller (turn)     Steer the camera / aim a drawn weapon - only the
                            controller's rotation counts, moving your hand does nothing
Right stick left/right      Turn your body (smooth turn)
Right stick up/down         Switch item / gadget              [Mouse wheel]
Right trigger               Fire / quick gadget               [Left mouse]
Left trigger                Alt-fire / quick gadget           [Right mouse]
Left stick                  Move                              [W A S D]
Left stick click            Walk / run                        [Middle mouse]
Right stick click           Draw / put away the weapon        [E]
Right grip                  Use / interact                    [Shift]
Left grip                   Roll / laser                      [Q]
A                           Jump                              [Space]
B                           Crouch                            [C]
Controller to your face     Binoculars on (hold the right controller up to your
                            eyes like binoculars); take it away = binoculars off  [B]
  while in binoculars:      look around with your HEAD, right stick up/down = zoom
X  (tap)                    Next gadget                       [Mouse wheel down]
X  (hold)                   Previous gadget                   [Mouse wheel up]
Y  (tap)                    Vision modes                      [Tab]
Y  (hold)                   Co-op move                        [T]
Menu button                 In-game menu                      [Esc]

Both stick clicks           First-person view on / off (EXPERIMENTAL - the view from
  (at the same time)        the spy's eyes; the game is not made for it and it can
                            glitch, click both again to go back to third person)

The Spy controls are used automatically whenever no first-person weapon is on screen.
The VR view stays steady behind the spy while the controller turns the camera: after
spawning, stand still for a moment and look around with the controller once so the mod
can learn the camera. Old mode (the right stick moves the camera like a mouse instead of
the controller): set SpyAim=0 in SCCT_VR.ini.


MENUS (main menu, match menus, in-game Esc menu)
------------------------------------------------
Left stick                  Move the selection                [Arrow keys]
A                           Confirm                           [Enter]
B                           Back                              [Esc]
Right stick                 Move the mouse cursor
Right trigger               Click                             [Left mouse]

The menu controls switch on and off automatically: as soon as a menu (including the
in-game menu during a match) is on screen the controller uses them, and when you leave it
the normal game controls come back - however you leave it (Back, A, B or the Menu button).


KEYBOARD (while playing in VR)
------------------------------
Home                        Recenter the view (holding the Meta button on the Quest also works)
End                         Controller aiming on / off
Insert                      HUD mode: follows the weapon <-> fixed in front of you
Num + / Num -               Bigger / smaller HUD (the value is written to SCCT_VR.log)
Pause                       Diagnostic frame dump (SCCT_VR_frame.txt)


CHANGING THE CONTROLS
---------------------
All of this can be changed in System\SCCT_VR.ini:
  [Bindings]      normal game controls (Mercenary)
  [SpyBindings]   overrides while playing the Spy
  [MenuBindings]  overrides in menus
Any control can also get a second action on a long press:  <Name>Hold=<key>
(for example YHold=T). ScopeKey=<key> in [Bindings] is the key the zoom gesture presses,
ReloadKey=<key> the key the reload gesture presses.
Control names: RightTrigger, LeftTrigger, RightGrip, LeftGrip,
A, B, X, Y, LeftStickClick, RightStickClick, Menu, LeftStickUp/Down/Left/Right,
RightStickUp/Down. Keys: a letter or digit, F1-F12, Space, Shift, Ctrl, Alt, Tab, Esc,
Enter, Backspace, Home, End, PgUp, PgDn, Up, Down, Left, Right, LMB, RMB, MMB,
WheelUp, WheelDown, or empty to unbind.
